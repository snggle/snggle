Privacy Policy
Effective date: 1 June 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Snggle Corporation Software Development (“Snggle Corp.,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Snggle Android app (the “App”).
This Privacy Policy also explains your privacy rights and how to contact us if you have questions or concerns.

2. Information processed on your device

The App processes information that you choose to enter or use in the App, such as:
- credentials or login records that you save in the App
- notes
- crypto transaction data that you create, import, scan, review, sign, or otherwise use in the App
- selected login data that you use with autofill through the App’s integration with the Android Autofill Framework
- selected data that you transfer using the App’s QR code feature
- app settings and preferences
- security data you set for the App, such as your PIN and master key or mnemonic

This information is processed locally on your device to provide the App’s features. In-app keyboard for sensitive fields: for certain sensitive fields, such as PINs and the master key / mnemonic, the App may provide an in-app keyboard instead of the device keyboard. Input entered through this in-app keyboard is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to Snggle Corp. or third parties. System keyboard note: If other fields use the system or user-selected keyboard on your device, that keyboard provider may process input according to its own privacy policy. Except where you intentionally use a feature described in this Privacy Policy that enters selected data into another app, website, service, or device, the App does not transmit App data outside your device.

3. Local storage, encryption, and device protection

Your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted app storage and Android platform security features. Access to the App may also be protected by security methods that you configure, such as PIN, master key and mnemonic. The App is configured to disable Android cloud backup for app data.
Because your data is stored only locally on your device and is not stored by Snggle Corp., we cannot recover it for you if you lose access to it. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and for remembering or safely storing your PIN, master key, mnemonic, and any other recovery information needed to access your data.

IMPORTANT: Because we do not store or sync your data, we cannot recover it for you. If you lose your device, uninstall the App, or forget your PIN/Master Key, your data will be permanently lost.

4. No server storage, analytics, ads, payments, or App data sharing

Snggle Corp. does not collect, store on its servers, sync, sell, rent, or otherwise share App data. The App does not automatically transmit App data to Snggle Corp. or to third parties. The App processes information that you choose to enter or use in the App, such as selected login data used with autofill through the App’s integration with the Android Autofill Framework. Selected data may leave the App only when you intentionally use QR code transfer or autofill. In those cases, the action is initiated by you, and the privacy practices of the recipient, target app, website, or service may apply. The App does not include analytics or tracking, does not use App data for advertising or profiling, does not show ads, and does not process in-app payments.
Please note that if you download or update the App through the Google Play Store, Google may process information in accordance with Google’s own privacy policy and terms. This processing is outside Snggle Corp.'s control.

5. QR code transfer

The App may allow you to generate or scan QR codes to transfer selected data between devices or to another app. QR code transfers are initiated by you, and you control whether a transfer takes place and what information is included. This processing is performed locally on your device. QR codes may be readable by any person, device, or application that is able to scan them. You should only generate, display, or share a QR code in a trusted environment. If you choose to transfer data using a QR code, you are responsible for checking that the receiving person, device, or app is trusted before completing the transfer. Once data is transferred to another device, application, or third-party service, that recipient’s privacy practices may apply.

6. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions needed for features that you choose to use, such as:
- Camera — used solely for scanning QR codes. The App processes the camera feed locally and in real-time; it does not capture, store, or transmit photos or videos. 

If you do not grant a permission, the related feature may not work. The App requests these permissions only to enable the corresponding feature you choose to use. You can also manage permissions in your Android device settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your App data at any time by:
- deleting items inside the App
- using the in-app "Wipe Application" feature
- clearing the App's storage in Android settings
- uninstalling the App

App Data Retention: Your App data is stored solely on your device. Because we do not host this data on our servers, we have no ability to remotely delete, retrieve, or restore it. Deletion and retention of App data stored on your device are entirely under your control.
Email Retention: We retain direct email correspondence only for as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry or to meet specific legal record-keeping obligations. Once that purpose is fulfilled, correspondence is deleted or anonymized.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be available inside the App and at the privacy policy URL linked from the Google Play listing. The “Effective Date” at the top of this Privacy Policy shows when the current version took effect. If we make material changes, we will update the Effective Date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice inside the App or through the Google Play listing.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the laws that apply to you, you may have the right to request access to personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion of that data, request restriction of processing, object to certain processing, and request data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may also have the right to withdraw consent at any time, although this will not affect processing already carried out before withdrawal. In practice, because Snggle Corp. does not store your data on its servers, rights relating to that data are mainly exercised by you directly on your device, for example by accessing, editing, exporting, or deleting the data in the App or by deleting the App’s storage.
To exercise any rights regarding personal data we do hold, such as support email correspondence, please contact us using the details provided in the “Contact” section of this Privacy Policy.

10. International data transfers

App Data: Snggle Corp. does not transmit your App data to its systems or servers, and we do not transfer App data internationally on your behalf. Selected App data may be received or processed in another country only if you intentionally use App features, such as QR code transfer or autofill through the App’s integration with the Android Autofill Framework, to send that data to another person, device, app, website, or service. Any such transfer is initiated and controlled by you, and the privacy practices of the recipient, target app, website, or service may apply.
Email Services: When you contact us by email, your message is processed through third-party email providers who may operate servers outside your home jurisdiction. Where applicable, we rely on appropriate safeguards made available by those providers, including contractual safeguards such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Automated decision-making and profiling

Snggle Corp. does not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

12. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age at which personal data processing requires parental authorization under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the App. If a child, parent, or guardian contacts us directly by email, we may process the information provided in that message as described in this Privacy Policy.

13. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle personal data, we encourage you to contact us first so that we can try to address your concern.
You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority or supervisory authority in your country or region.

14. Contact

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message, and use it only to respond to your inquiry. Please do not send highly sensitive data, PINs, master keys, mnemonics, or full passwords to us.
